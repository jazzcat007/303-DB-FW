STUDIO BOY + TECHNO BOY + GRANULAR BOY + AMBIENT BOY + BERLIN BOY + APHEX BOY + LAUNCH BOY - SD CARD IMAGE (19 Nov 2026)

Make a card:
1. Format the SD card as FAT32.
2. Copy everything in this folder to the card's root (this README can be left out).
3. Put the card in the box and power on. The box's bootloader installs Launch Boy
   (it then moves the file to System/Firmware), and the Launch Boy menu appears.
4. First time the menu says NOT WHOLE: choose a firmware with + (key 8) / - (key 20),
   press ||| (key 14) to start it. It is copied in (5-8 s) and starts.
   At every power-on after that the menu lets you pick Studio Boy, Techno Boy, Granular Boy, Ambient Boy,
   Berlin Boy or Aphex Boy.

What is here:
  Drumboy_Pro_Fw_LB_1_03_nor.bin   Launch Boy 1.03 (the only Drumboy_Pro_Fw file allowed in the root)
  FIRMWARE/StudioBoy_4_98_lboy.bin Studio Boy 4.98 for Launch Boy
  FIRMWARE/TechnoBoy_9_93_lboy.bin Techno Boy 9.93 for Launch Boy (not yet tried on the box)
  FIRMWARE/GranularBoy_0_08_lboy.bin Granular Boy 0.08 for Launch Boy (not yet tried on the box)
  FIRMWARE/AmbientBoy_0_05_lboy.bin Ambient Boy 0.05 for Launch Boy (not yet tried on the box)
  FIRMWARE/BerlinBoy_0_13_lboy.bin  Berlin Boy 0.13 for Launch Boy
  FIRMWARE/AphexBoy_0_02_lboy.bin   Aphex Boy 0.02 for Launch Boy (not yet tried on the box)
  FIRMWARE/LAUNCH_BOY_LICENSE.md   Launch Boy's licence (PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0)
  Groovebox/                       Studio Boy's kits, samples and 31 demo pattern slots
  GranularBoy/Samples/             Granular Boy's 36 demo samples
  AmbientBoy/                      Ambient Boy's folder (its seeds, knob map and REC takes are written here)
  BerlinBoy/Samples/               Berlin Boy's drum kits: 909, 808, 727, 606 (Pads/, Sets/, MidiMaps/ are made by the box)
  AphexBoy/Samples/                Aphex Boy's drum kits: 909, 808, 727, 606 (Textures/, Vocals/ for your samples; Pads/,
                                   Sets/, MidiMaps/ are made by the box)
  TechnoBoy/Pads/                  Techno Boy's ten pad samples (MidiMaps/ and Sets/ are made by the box)
  System/                          the Drumboy Pro board's own files (must be present)
  LICENSE.txt                      Studio Boy's freeware licence

Going back to one firmware: put a normal Drumboy_Pro_Fw_..._nor.bin (or Studio Boy's
Drumboy_Pro_Fw_04_98.bin) alone in the root and power on.
