NOT A GROOVEBOX - PUBLIC FOLDERS

The Drumboy Pro apps by 303 Resistance, free (each under its own licence, in its folder or source zip).
Each app's folder holds its standard firmware (for the box's stock bootloader), its User Guide and, where given,
its source code; Launch Boy With All Apps is one card with every app on it, picked from Launch Boy's menu at power-on.

  Aphex Boy 0.02            APHEX: generative glitch electronica after Aphex Twin - polymeter, drills, grains;
                            the MIXER, drum kits, pads, scenes, MIDI MAP (not yet tried on the box; no User Guide yet:
                            its Documentation folder has the key maps and the MIDI numbers)
                            Source code: full (the whole repo at the 0.02 release)
  Ambient Boy 0.03          AMBIENT DRIFT: generative ambient, live grains
                            Source code: full (the whole repo at the 0.03 release, with the licence)
  Berlin Boy 0.12           BERLIN: generative Berlin techno, the MIXER, drum kits, pads, scenes, MIDI MAP
                            Source code: full (the whole repo at the 0.12 release)
  Granular Boy 0.06         a granular instrument from your samples
                            Source code: full (the whole repo at the 0.06 release, with the licence)
  Studio Boy 4.92           the full studio: 303, drums, ACID SYNTH, sequencers, sampler
                            Source code: partial - the instruments and effects only: the 303 voice and its circuit
                            model, ACID SYNTH, the 909-style drum machine and its kits; Drive, Phaser, Delay,
                            Flanger, Reverb, DubEcho, DjFilter, the compressor and ducker, Scatter, beat repeat and
                            tape stop, Granulator, Vocoder, Talkbox, AutoTune, NoiseGate (its README.md lists every
                            file)
  Techno Boy 9.92           APHEX, BERLIN and INSANE mixed live
                            Source code: full (the whole repo: the 9.92 release plus the PC app's key-map overlay)
  Launch Boy With All Apps  every app on one card, with Launch Boy's boot menu
                            Source code: Launch Boy's own, full (Source Code\LaunchBoy_1_01_source_b45a5ad.zip:
                            the boot menu, its packaging tools, the PC's virtual Drumboy Pro, how to make a firmware
                            for Launch Boy); it builds with the board code in Techno Boy's source

To install one app: copy its SD Card folder's contents (for Aphex Boy its Firmware folder's, for Studio Boy its
firmware file) to the root of a FAT32 microSD card, keep one Drumboy_Pro_Fw file at the root, and power on. The folders' README files say more.
