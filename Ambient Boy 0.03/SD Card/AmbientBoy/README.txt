AMBIENT BOY's folder. The box writes its own files here:

  SEEDS.AMB      the seeds kept on the ten pads (5 KEEP; on MIX, 19 empties the picked pad)
  KNOBMAP.AMB    the controller's knobs paired with the box's (24 LEARN)
  RECORDINGS/    REC001.WAV on: each take of 3 REC (stereo, 16-bit, 44.1 kHz)

Delete SEEDS.AMB or KNOBMAP.AMB to start again with empty pads or no pairings.
