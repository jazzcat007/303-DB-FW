TECHNO BOY 9.92 - for the Drumboy Pro's stock bootloader (not Launch Boy)

  SD Card\        copy everything in it to the root of a FAT32 microSD card:
                    Drumboy_Pro_Fw_TB_9_92_nor.bin   the firmware (keep one Drumboy_Pro_Fw file at the card's root)
                    TechnoBoy\Pads\                  empty: put your own WAVs here for the pads (read at power-on)
                  Techno Boy needs no samples to play (APHEX, BERLIN and INSANE make their own sounds). It makes
                  TechnoBoy\MidiMaps (the MIDI MAP's files) and TechnoBoy\Sets (REC, key 3) itself.
  User Guide\     Techno Boy 9.92 - User Guide.pdf
  Source Code\    TechnoBoy_9_92_source_67d4d78.zip - the whole repo at commit 67d4d78 (notagroovebox/techno-boy):
                  the 9.92 firmware plus the PC app's key-map overlay since
